HTML5 ke Superhero Tags: Semantic Tags

Scene 1: Problem - Jab Sab Kuch "Dabba" Tha

Maan lo tum naye ghar me shift hue. Saare dabbe pe kuch likha hi nahi hai.

1 dabbe me bartan, 1 me kapde, 1 me books. Par upar sab pe likha hai "Saman".

Ab mummy bolegi "beta kitab wala dabba dena". Tum 1 ghante tak sab dabbe khologe. Takleef hui na?

Bilkul aise hi pehle HTML tha. Developer har cheez ko <div> naam ke dabbe me daal deta tha.

Header bhi div, Footer bhi div, Menu bhi div. Browser bechara har baar saare dabbe khol kar dekhta tha "is dabbe me kya hai?"

Scene 2: Entry - HTML5 ke Superhero

Phir HTML5 ke superhero aaye aur bole "bas ab aur nahi".

Unhone har dabbe pe naam likh diya. Ab kitab wale dabbe pe "Kitab", bartan wale pe "Bartan".

Header ka naam <header>, Menu ka naam <nav>, Article ka naam <article>

Inhi naam wale dabbo ko hum Semantic Tags bolte hain. "Semantic" = "Jiska matlab ho"

Ab browser ko 1 second me pata chal jata hai "acha ye header hai, ye main content hai"

Scene 4: 9 Superhero - Naam, Kaam, Power

1. <header> = Dukaan ka Board

Power: Logo aur naam dikhata hai. Sabse upar rehta hai.

2. <nav> = Mall ka Map

Power: Saare important links yahi milenge. Bina bhatke har jagah ja sakte ho.

3. <main> = Movie ki Kahani

Power: Ye sabse important hai. 1 page = 1 hi main. Duplicate nahi chalega.

4. <article> = Newspaper

Power: Khud me complete hai. Ise kaat ke kahin bhi chipka do, phir bhi chalega.

5. <section> = Kitaab ke Chapter

Power: Bade topic ko tukdo me todta hai. Har section ka apna heading hota hai.

6. <aside> = TV ke Side me Ads

Power: Main kahani se related hai par zaroori nahi. Pro Tip, Ads yahi aate hain.

7. <footer> = Film ke End Credits

Power: Sabse niche. Copyright, Contact yahi milta hai.

8. <figure> + <figcaption> = Photo + Neeche Caption

Power: Google ko batata hai "ye photo kis cheez ki hai". SEO boost.

9. <time> = Calendar

Power: Machine ko date samjhata hai. Google ko pata chalta hai content naya hai.

Scene 5: Climax - div vs section ka Jhagda Khatam

<div> = Sirf ek dabba. Na naam, na kaam. Bas saman bharne ke liye.

Example: Website me 2 button ko saath me rakhna hai. To unhe <div> me daal do.

<section> = Naam wala dabba. Uspe title chipka hai.

Example: "About Us" wala hissa, "Services" wala hissa. Har ek ka heading hoga.

Golden Rule yaad rakh:

Agar us hisse ka upar heading ban sakta hai jaise <h2>About Us</h2> to <section> use karo.

Agar heading ka koi kaam nahi hai, bas design ke liye dabba chahiye to <div> use karo.
